## Services
1. **Bone Fracture Detection**: Users can upload X-ray images, and the AI (YOLO11) detects fractures with high accuracy in seconds.
2. **AI-Generated Clinical Reports**: Automatically generates a detailed, AI-driven PDF report via Llama 3.2.
3. **Clinical AI Summary**: Each report includes a clinical-style narrative of the findings.
4. **Grad-CAM Visualizations**: Heatmaps showing exactly where the AI "looked" at the image.
5. **Doctor Consultations**: Direct appointment booking with orthopedic specialists.
6. **Diagnosis History**: A secure record of all past scans and reports.

## Pricing & Credits (Pay as you Wish)
Orthonx has moved to a simplified, transparent pricing model known as **Pay as you Wish**.
- **No Fixed Packages**: We have removed all pre-set packages (Starter, Standard, Premium).
- **Flexible Top-up**: Users can buy any custom amount of credits they need.
- **Minimum Transaction**: ₨ 100.
- **Conversion Rate**: 1 Credit = ₨ 2 (Pakistani Rupees).
- **Validity**: Credits are typically valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
- **Cost per Diagnosis**: Standardly ~10-20 credits per analysis.
